## Summary

Phase 13.1 goal fully achieved. Both plans executed cleanly:

**Plan 01:** MACEDipoleCalculator gained calculate_dipole_derivatives() (autograd, replaces
2x3N finite-difference passes) and calculate_polarizability() (direct model forward pass,
returns (3,3) Angstrom^3 or zeros). The _last_dalpha_dr side-effect attribute threads
polarizability Jacobian data downstream. No silent fallback to finite differences — exceptions
propagate loudly as designed.

**Plan 02:** calculate_dipole_properties() expanded from 3-tuple to 4-tuple returning
polarizability_voigt6 in Bohr^3. Unit conversion (Angstrom^3 -> Bohr^3) centralised in
workflow.py via ANGSTROM3_TO_BOHR3 constant. dalpha_dr threaded into mol.info["dalpha_dr"]
for future Raman analysis (Python-pipeline-only — not written to Gaussian output).
write_gaussian_output() now accepts real polarizability values with None-default backward
compatibility.
