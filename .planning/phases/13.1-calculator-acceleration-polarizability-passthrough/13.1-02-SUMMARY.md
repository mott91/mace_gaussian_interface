## Decisions Made

- dalpha_dr stored in mol.info["dalpha_dr"] but NOT written to Gaussian output file -- it is Python-pipeline-only data for downstream Raman analysis
- Voigt 6-component packing [axx, axy, ayy, axz, ayz, azz] matches Gaussian's documented convention
